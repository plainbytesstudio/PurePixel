# Privacy Policy for PurePixel

**Effective Date: March 29, 2026**

**PlainBytes Studio** ("we," "us," or "our") develops the **PurePixel** application (the "Software"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Software.

---

### 1. The Core Principle: 100% Offline Processing
PurePixel is built on a "Privacy-First" architecture. **All image processing operations—including format conversion, resizing, and watermarking—are performed locally on your own computer.** Your original images, processed results, and image metadata are never uploaded to any server or cloud service.

### 2. Information Collection and Use
*   **Image Data:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your images. Your files never leave your device.
*   **Personal Data:** PurePixel does not require account registration. We do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
*   **Usage Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs (such as Google Analytics) to monitor your behavior or collect telemetry data within the application.

### 3. Third-Party Services: Microsoft Store
PurePixel is distributed through the Microsoft Store and includes In-App Purchases (IAP) for Professional features.
*   **Payment Processing:** All financial transactions and billing information are handled exclusively by **Microsoft**. We do not have access to, nor do we store, your credit card details or payment information.
*   **License Validation:** Upon startup, the Software calls official Microsoft Store APIs to verify your Pro license status. This communication happens through encrypted system channels managed by the Windows operating system.

### 4. Application Permissions
*   **Internet Access:** The Software requests network access solely for the purpose of validating your license status and processing Pro version upgrades via the Microsoft Store. No other background data transmission occurs.
*   **File System Access:** The Software only accesses the specific files and folders you explicitly select (via drag-and-drop or the file picker) to perform requested image operations.

### 5. Data Security
Because we do not collect or store your data on any remote servers, there is no risk of your personal images being compromised through a data breach of our systems. The security of your local files is protected by your own computer's operating system security settings.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our official GitHub repository or our official website.
